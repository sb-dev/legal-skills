## 1. Scope and questions

1. Which SKILL.md-based legal skills (and comparable agent instruction packages) already exist; what do they do; do they verify authority, handle jurisdiction, date their law — or are they templates and prompts?
2. Which legal MCP servers / connectors exist for legislation, case law, registries, trade marks and contracts; who runs them; under what licence; are they usable for UK / EU work?
3. Which commercial legal-AI providers matter as things to REFERENCE or integrate (not rebuild); UK/EU coverage; API availability; vendor accuracy claims versus independent evaluation.
4. Which evaluation frameworks and datasets exist; licences; what they measure and what they do not; which can be USED or ADAPTED for Stage 18.

Out of scope here (other assignments): the primary data sources themselves (legislation.gov.uk, Find Case Law, EUR-Lex, registries) except where a licence term directly constrains a skill or MCP server assessed below.

**What no benchmark found here measures** (each is a Stage 18 build item, not an adoption):

1. **Currency** — whether the answer reflects the law in force at the as-at date, including commencement, prospective provisions and unapplied amendments. No dataset carries an as-at date per item.
2. **Jurisdiction fit** (Stage 5 V6) — whether an authority from the wrong territory / extent / forum was used. VLAIR touches multi-state difficulty only.
3. **Later treatment** (V5) — nobody tests for reliance on overruled or superseded authority outside closed vendor products.
4. **Product-to-law consistency and change impact** — no dataset pairs a product artefact (code, data flow, UI copy, policy) with the legal text it must match.
5. **Escalation / refusal quality** — whether the system stops, discloses limits, or routes to a human at the right point. VLAIR records "unable to respond" but does not score appropriate escalation.
6. **Confidentiality behaviour** — whether matter facts leaked into search queries or third-party tools.
7. **England-and-Wales and EU-level law** — no English-law practice benchmark was found. EU coverage exists only as classification (LexGLUE EUR-LEX, MultiEURLEX) or exams (LEXam).

### 4.2 Genuinely missing in the landscape (within what was searched)

1. A **shared authority record** with separate existence / text / support / currency / treatment / jurisdiction-fit results and derived gates (Stage 5 §6). Nearest: J-14's graded verdicts, UK only, V1–V2 only.
2. A **temporal-validity contract** applied to every proposition. Nearest: `skills-qa` freshness fields — for bundled reference files, not for propositions.
3. **Legal production for builders**: inputs that are code, data flows, UI copy, pricing pages, SBOMs; outputs that are product changes plus legal artefacts kept consistent with each other. Nothing found.
4. **Product-to-law traceability and change impact** (Stage 8). Nothing found beyond regulation-vs-policy diffs.
5. **Confidentiality routing by sensitivity class and most-exposed tool** (Stage 10). Nothing found; claude-for-legal ships a dozen third-party connectors per plugin with no visible routing step.
6. **An England-and-Wales / EU-first open pack under a permissive licence.** Forks exist for CN, TR, FI, ES, DE; none for E&W. The only E&W pack is FSL-licensed.
7. **An official or operator-run MCP for legislation.gov.uk, Find Case Law or EUR-Lex.** Anthropic lists it as wanted (J-04). Community servers exist; J-25 (EUR-Lex) is the only one that looks fit to test.
8. **Benchmarks for currency, jurisdiction fit, escalation and confidentiality behaviour** (§3.4).

### 4.3 Consequence for positioning

`legal-skills` should present itself as complementary to claude-for-legal — a verification, temporal and product-consistency layer with a UK/EU source profile, installable beside it — and should be built to pass `legal-builder-hub`'s trust and freshness gates. Competing on breadth of practice-area skills would be rebuilding what exists.
